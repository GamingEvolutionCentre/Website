## Information collected

You do not need an account or sign-in to browse this website. If you only browse the site, I do not receive your name or email address from you.

If you use the support form, I collect:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your support, help or complaint message

This information is used only to receive, respond to and resolve your request. The lawful basis for this processing is **legitimate interests**.

## Storage and third parties

Support requests are sent and stored by email and are kept until the request, problem or complaint has been resolved. Only I can access the support mailbox.

The website uses:

- **Cloudflare** for website hosting and support-form processing. Cloudflare may process submitted form data, your IP address and normal request, security and diagnostic information needed to operate and protect the website.
- **IONOS** for email delivery and storage. IONOS may process your submitted contact details and message, together with normal email routing, delivery and security information.

This website does not use analytics or tracking cookies.

## Your UK GDPR rights

If you submit personal information through the support form, your rights under the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018 may include asking to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, objecting to certain processing, and complaining to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Some rights depend on the circumstances and legal basis for processing.
