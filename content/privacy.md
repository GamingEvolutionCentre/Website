Last updated: **5 October 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Gaming Evolution Centre collects through the website, why it is collected, how it is handled, and the rights you have under UK data protection law.

## What data is collected

When you use the support form, the following information may be collected:

- Your name
- Your email address
- The support, help or complaint message you submit

## Why this information is collected

This information is collected so that I can read your support request, respond to you, and help resolve the problem or complaint you submitted.

## How your information is sent and stored

Support form information is sent by email. The website uses Cloudflare to handle the website and support form request, and IONOS to send and store the support email.

Your support information is therefore processed through these services only as needed to deliver the support request and email.

## How long support emails are kept

Support emails are kept until the help request, problem or complaint has been resolved.

Once the matter has been resolved, the information is no longer needed for the purpose of handling that support request.

## Who can access your information

Only I can access the support information sent to Gaming Evolution Centre through the support email account.

Cloudflare and IONOS may process the information as part of providing the website infrastructure and email services used to deliver your support request.

## Analytics and cookies

Gaming Evolution Centre does not use website analytics and does not use cookies to track visitors.

## Third-party services and data processing

Gaming Evolution Centre uses third-party services to operate the website and deliver support requests. These providers may process personal information and technical information only as needed to provide their services.

### Cloudflare

Cloudflare is used to host and deliver the website and to run the support form function.

When you visit the website or submit the support form, Cloudflare may process information needed to deliver and protect the service. This can include:

- The name, email address and support message you submit through the support form
- Your IP address
- Basic request information such as the page or web address requested
- Browser, device or connection information contained in normal web requests
- Security, performance and diagnostic information generated while operating and protecting the website

Cloudflare is used to receive the support form request and pass the submitted information to the email-sending process. Gaming Evolution Centre does not use Cloudflare analytics to track visitors.

### IONOS

IONOS provides the email service used for **support@gamingevolutioncentre.co.uk**.

When a support request is sent by email, IONOS may process information needed to send, receive, protect and store that email. This can include:

- Your name and email address where included in the support request
- The contents of your support, help or complaint message
- The sender and recipient email addresses
- Standard email header and routing information
- Technical mail-server, security and delivery log information generated as part of providing the email service

The support email is stored in the IONOS email account until the help request, problem or complaint has been resolved.

Cloudflare and IONOS have their own privacy and data-protection obligations for the information they process while providing their services.

## Your UK GDPR rights

Even though Gaming Evolution Centre only asks for limited personal information through the support form, such as your name, email address and support message, that information is still personal data. UK data-protection rights may therefore apply.

Under the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018, you may have rights over your personal information, including the right to:

- Ask for a copy of the personal information held about you
- Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected
- Ask for your personal information to be deleted in certain circumstances
- Ask for the use of your information to be restricted in certain circumstances
- Object to certain uses of your personal information
- Ask for your information to be provided in a portable format where applicable
- Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent
- Make a complaint to the Information Commissioner's Office if you believe your data protection rights have been breached

Some rights depend on the circumstances and the legal basis for processing.
