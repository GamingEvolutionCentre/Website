## Returns

Eligible items can be returned within **30 days of delivery**.

To request a return, email **support@gamingevolutioncentre.co.uk**.

Where applicable, returned items should be in the condition received, unused or unworn, with their original packaging and proof of purchase.

## UK cancellation rights

Any statutory cancellation or refund rights you have under UK consumer law are not affected by this policy. Different rules or exceptions may apply depending on the type of product, including digital content.

## Refunds

If a refund is approved, it will be returned to your original payment method, normally within **10 business days**. Your bank or payment provider may take additional time to process it.

If more than **15 business days** have passed after your refund was approved and you have not received it, email **support@gamingevolutioncentre.co.uk**.

For any return or refund questions, contact **support@gamingevolutioncentre.co.uk**.
